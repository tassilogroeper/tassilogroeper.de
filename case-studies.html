Guidance Application — Roche, Basel

2022 · Tech lead & main developer · Full-stack (Laravel/Symfony, Vue.js, API)

Problem

A global pharmaceutical company managed cross-border engagement, grant, sponsorship and donation guidance in scattered country-specific documents — inconsistent formats, no comparability, no guarantee that information was current, and no access for external partners.

Solution

  • Architected and built a central guidance platform from a pilot concept: discovery, information architecture, MVP planning, delivery.
  • Flexible calculation engine for Fair Market Values and engagement compensation, supporting country-specific compliance rules.
  • Country-autonomous back office: admins and authors manage their own country's content; legal-audit workflow built in.

Result

The platform became the single, scalable starting point for all engagement and GSD activities across countries — replacing document chaos with a searchable, compliance-aware application.

LENA — NLP Customer-Service Framework, with PIDAS

2019 · Full-stack developer · Client: Sympany (among others)

Problem

Service teams needed to design complex customer conversations — including AI chat, live-chat escalation and transactional processes — without engineering support for every change.

Solution

  • Built a web framework for conversation workflows: intuitive backend for configuring workflows and training sessions.
  • Hybrid model: AI conversations with human live-chat support; data visualisation to analyse user needs and iterate conversations quickly.

Result

Customers could create, brand and operate best-in-class chatbot experiences themselves — AI and human hand-in-hand.
